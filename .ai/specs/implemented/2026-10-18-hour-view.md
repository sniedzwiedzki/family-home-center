# Hour view of one expanded day

## Goal
One day at a time is expanded into an hour view; the rest stay compact rows.

## Rules
- Default hours 7:00-20:00 (local time); widened to whole hours only for timed events starting earlier or ending later (clipped at midnight).
- Timed events sit at their hours in their person column; overlapping events in a column share the width in lanes. All-day events and continuation days stay in the compact row above the hour grid.
- A now-line (with the time) on today moves in 5-minute steps.
- Default expanded day: today, until it is over (now is past 20:00 and past the end of its last timed event), then the next day.
- Tapping a date toggles that day; expanding one collapses the other; a collapsed state is allowed (nothing expanded). The choice only holds on the day it was made.
- A home button in the header's day label column drops the choice and scrolls to the default expanded day.
- The pinned copy of today stays compact and is not expandable.

## Verification
Unit tests (100% coverage) and the integration test "expands one day at a time into hours" with a 1600 x 2400 screenshot.
