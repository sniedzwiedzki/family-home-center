# Day row UI

Date: 2026-10-17
Status: in progress

## Requirements (from the user)

1. The month name is a vertical label on the left, acting as its own column.
2. The date number (e.g. 25) uses a larger font; the weekday is a short two-letter abbreviation: po, wt, śr, cz, pt, so, nd.
3. Every Saturday has its own style and every Sunday has its own style; Monday to Friday share one style.

## Decisions

- Days are grouped into month blocks. Each block has a left column with the month name set vertically (reads bottom to top) that stays visible (sticky) while the month scrolls. The column headers are offset by the same width so columns stay aligned.
- Month label: name only ("październik"); January also shows the year ("styczeń 2027") so long scrolls stay unambiguous.
- Weekday abbreviations come from a fixed map (Intl gives "pon.", "czw.", ... which is not the two-letter form requested).
- Saturday: cool blue tint, blue date; Sunday: warm red tint, red date; Mon-Fri: neutral. "Today" keeps its own accent (accent-coloured date and an inset accent bar) on top of the weekday style.
- A month block is at least 10rem tall so a one-day partial month still shows its name.
- The full-day label `formatDayLabel` ("sobota, 17 października") is removed; nothing uses it any more.
